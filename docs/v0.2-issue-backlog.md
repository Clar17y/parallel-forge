## Accepted personal-use provider policy

The 24 September operator-trust policy supersedes the older proof-first queue.
`FORGE_SUBSCRIPTION_CLIENT_TRUST=operator` is sufficient for configured personal
Codex, Claude, legacy Gemini ACP and Antigravity clients. Missing capability
reports, provider-internal billing attestations or per-version native-tool proof
are not release prerequisites. Optional `verified` admission remains separate.

Retain `operator_trusted` / **Ready to attempt** and Antigravity's
`approved_tools_unproved` warning. Actual route/task/callback checks, credential
filtering, named tools, ownership, budgets, quota and stop settlement remain.
An approved run can delegate within its frozen routes and budgets without a new
proof workflow per child. Trust does not excuse a known functional failure.

DeepSeek and new paid-API integration are outside this work. Where configured,
Codex subscription `gpt-6-luna` / medium is the routine fallback. Intentional
one-run profile and named task-route overrides are
[#31](https://github.com/Clar17y/parallel-forge/issues/31), explicitly post-v0.2.
